An illustrated anime cinematic featuring Chaos, Hunter and Gold Mage. From the neon streets to the rooftops, canals and casino: steal the archive key, survive the pursuit, make your own exit. Game visuals differ from this opening.

Story and audio description

A train crosses the bay as Crown City lights up. Street traffic passes the late-night food stalls. Chaos meets Hunter in the garage; their target is the Palace archive key. A red muscle car leads the police across the bridge. Chaos sprints across rooftops, leaps and returns fire, then escapes through the canal and above the city. Gold Mage waits in the casino tower. A motorcycle chase and a rooftop explosion give way to dawn. Chaos stands over the city: make your own exit.

The original score, Neon Debt, moves from quiet keys to guitars, synths and drums at 144 BPM. Engines, sirens, footsteps, shots, water and rotors follow the action. There is no spoken dialogue.
